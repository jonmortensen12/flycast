Left trigger · three stages

Off — line runs free from the reel through the guides.

Half — line routes through your hand but slides freely. This is how you hold it between casts.

Full — line is pinched. Hand toward the tip pays out, hand away strips in.

Slack

Line you strip in pools at your feet — it does not go back on the reel.

Shooting feeds from the pool first, then off the reel. The right trigger winds the pool back on before it moves the fly.

Seeing things

Flecks on the surface drift at the true local current. Thin fast water carries them; the pool barely moves.

The fish wears a marker, and every zone that governs his decision is drawn on the water.

Y opens the constants menu — 23 values now, including everything the fish uses to judge you. He starts forgiving. Tighten him up once the casting feels right.

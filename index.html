Grabbing line

You now grab the nearest point on the line, wherever your hand is. Grab down by the reel and pull for maximum payout.

No direction to get right — it's tension on each side of your fingers that decides which way line moves.

The cork clamp

Right grip traps the line against the cork. Release it and the line is completely free from spool to stripping guide.

Specks

Up to 4000, with size and spread controls. They recycle from a fixed pool, so they can't pile up.

Some respawn upstream, some seed on a moving grid around you — including in the dead water behind rocks.

Fixed

Line no longer snags on invisible points in the air while reeling. That was stale geometry, not physics.

Eddies behind the rocks now genuinely run backwards, so specks caught in them circulate instead of streaming past.

Test rig 08 · spool to fly, one chain · build 2026-08-08s

Flycast
one line

One discretisation

34 m of line at a fixed node spacing. Node i is always the same piece of line — nothing is created or destroyed, ever.

Node length is a setting. Watch the FPS and node count and find your ceiling.

Guides are constraints

All eleven eyelets pin the line two-way, so line inside the rod has real mass and pulls back on the blank.

Off the reel

Line pays off only when the spool side is taut and you exceed a deadband. Then it comes 1:1 with your hand.

Each click of the spool is a haptic tick.

Stripping is the same rule

Pull harder on the slack than the rod side and line slides back in through the guides. Same friction, reversed.

Nothing pushes the line anywhere. Every metre that leaves the spool or the tiptop does so because a friction threshold was exceeded by tension you created.

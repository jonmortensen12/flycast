the run

Moving

Left stick walks. You can wade in — the bed drops away and deep water slows you down.

You can't go over about chest deep.

Line hands

Right side button traps the line against the cork. Everything above the pool locks.

Left trigger: half routes, full pinches. Right trigger reels.

The net

Left side button brings up the net. Get a tired fish inside it to land him.

Reading fish

Feeding trout nose the surface for naturals. Those are the ones that will eat.

A fly landing above a fish can drift down into him — no need to drop it in a box.

Y opens the menu. Line mass now defaults to 1.85 and the line runs on 96 segments with a smoothed render, so the tip should stop looking like linked sticks.
